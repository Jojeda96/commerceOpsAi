# Recommendation Evidence Policy — CommerceOps AI V4.2

## Epistemic Classes of Recommendations

Every recommendation generated by the Strategy Agent is validated and classified into one of four epistemic kinds:

1. **`EVIDENCE_BACKED_ACTION`**: Directly supported by active, approved quantitative findings.
2. **`HYPOTHESIS_TO_TEST`**: Proposed intervention requiring external data or future pilot validation not contained in historical dataset.
3. **`MONITORING_ACTION`**: Scheduled or periodic monitoring proposal.
4. **`DATA_QUALITY_ACTION`**: Action aimed at improving data coverage or snapshot population.

## Prohibited Claims

- **Real-Time Claims**: Replaced by "monitoreo periódico" as long as real-time ingestion capabilities are disabled.
- **Unverified Causal Impact**: Reductions like "reducirá 26.3%" without quantitative simulation are prohibited.
